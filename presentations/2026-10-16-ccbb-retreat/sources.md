## From Marcus directly (not independently confirmed)

- AI Governance Committee members come from IUSM, IU Health and Regenstrief; it acts as an advisory group on running an AI research program the right way.
- Dean's office AI group includes David Hanson, director of artificial intelligence; it supports AI in clinical and education settings with proof-of-concept development and consulting.
- The Northwest-Gary joint consult (ODSAI and the Dean's office AI group met with the faculty members about feasibility and implementation options).
- Dayu Sun (Biostatistics) is developing an AI Literacy course; ODSAI coordinates across classes with similar needs.
- AI resource rules: ChatGPT Edu, Gemini and Copilot can be used with restricted data; REALLMS is the only option approved for PHI; ChatGPT Edu includes Codex but its monthly credits go fast; Copilot (Azure) can have costs; Jetstream2 is for open research with no PHI or critical data, and has API access.
- REALLMS models: gpt-oss-120b, Qwen3.8-27B, glm5.3-flash (coming soon). IU's catalog page doesn't list models.
- Workshop format: three weekly 3.5-hour sessions, 20–25 participants with TAs, a personal VM using REALLMS.
