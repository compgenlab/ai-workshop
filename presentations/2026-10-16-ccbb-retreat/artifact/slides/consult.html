One example of a joint consult: a group at IU School of Medicine–Northwest-Gary wanted to use AI tools to capture conversations in a clinical learning environment, and ODSAI and the Dean’s office AI group met with the faculty members to discuss feasibility and implementation options for their project. This is only an example; the outcome is not part of the talk. ODSAI’s focus is supporting the needs of basic and computational labs; helping with clinical projects like this one is a bonus.
