CCBB Retreat 2026

About 30 seconds. ODSAI is IUSM’s office to help researchers accelerate their research using AI: not just learning about AI, but learning how to apply it directly to their own work. IU’s AI Research Network is big and covers all of IU; ODSAI is small and can be more hands-on when needed. IUSM also has separate efforts on using AI in clinical education, driven by Anthony Shanks; those are out of scope for this talk. Per the IUSM MD student news (Mar 2, 2026), AI content is being added to Phase 1 health systems science courses, fourth-year specialty electives are in development, and a two-year AI scholarly concentration pilot has been proposed.
