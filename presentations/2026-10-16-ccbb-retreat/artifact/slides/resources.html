About 1 minute. The AI resources IUSM researchers can use today. ChatGPT Edu (IU license) and Google Gemini, including Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM, renamed July 2026), are best for general chat and light coding. ChatGPT Edu, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot can all be used with restricted data. ChatGPT Edu includes Codex for coding, but use is limited by its free monthly credits, which go fast; Copilot (through Azure) can have some cost associated. NSF resources, Jetstream2 and NRP (the National Research Platform), are great for projects that fit their allocation requirements. Jetstream2 is primarily an NSF production cloud for open, publishable, or general academic and scientific research, so no PHI or critical data. Jetstream2 also offers API access; NRP offers free LLM access for researchers and educators through a chat interface and an OpenAI-compatible API (https://nrp.ai/llms). REALLMS is the only one approved for PHI; details on the next slide.
