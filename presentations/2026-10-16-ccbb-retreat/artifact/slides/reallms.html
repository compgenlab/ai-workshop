About 1 minute. REALLMS (Research and Academic LLM Services) is IU’s on-premise LLM service, run by UITS Research Technologies in the IU Data Center and free to the IU community. It is the only IU option approved for PHI. Current models: gpt-oss-120b and Qwen3.8-27B, with glm5.3-flash coming soon. Access is through a chat interface or an API (through RT projects). This is also what the workshop VMs will use. Source: https://researchdata.iu.edu/resource-catalog/about-reallms-at-iu/index.html
