CCBB Retreat 2026

About 2 minutes. Three places to go for help with AI in research. ODSAI is IUSM’s office to help you accelerate your research using AI. Its focus is supporting the use of AI to accelerate biomedical research, both basic and computational, and education is a main pillar of that work: teaching researchers how to actively apply these tools to their research today (the workshop later in this talk is one example). It is small enough to be hands-on, helping researchers apply AI directly to their own work, and connecting them onward when needed. The Fall 2025 IUSM All School Meeting recap says an AI Governance Committee has been formed, made up of education, clinical, research and IT leaders, to focus on policies and guidance for faculty, staff and students; its members come from IUSM, IU Health and Regenstrief; for researchers it acts as an advisory group on the logistics of running an AI research program the right way (which tools are approved, handling protected data, compliance, who to talk to). The Dean’s office AI group, which includes David Hanson, director of artificial intelligence, supports AI in clinical and education settings through proof-of-concept development and consulting.
